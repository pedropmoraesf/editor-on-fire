AutoFX attribution
==================

The high-quality tone estimator in tone_analysis_hq.c uses signal-analysis and
feature-layout concepts derived from the AutoFX project:

  AutoFX - Automatic Effect Recognition and Configuration for Timbre Reproduction
  Copyright (c) 2022 Sony CSL Paris
  https://github.com/SonyCSLParis/AutoFX

AutoFX is distributed under the MIT License.  Its trained classifier checkpoint
and scaler are not distributed by that repository, so EOF does not include or
claim to reproduce the original trained model.  EOF independently implements a
C feature extractor inspired by the published AutoFX feature organization and
uses a separate deterministic classifier adapted to mixed full-song audio.
